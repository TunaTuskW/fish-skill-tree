## 4. Channel translation rules

Never paste identical copy across every channel.

- **Website / Landing page:** Five-second hero clarity -> proof above the fold -> workflow walkthrough -> objection FAQ -> specific CTA.
- **LinkedIn:** Contrarian observation or concrete field lesson -> breakdown with numbers or real tradeoffs -> single question or direct takeaway.
- **X / Threads:** Hook in sentence 1 (specific number, artifact, or broken assumption) -> tight mechanism steps -> concrete receipt.
- **Email / Outbound:** Specific trigger why reaching out now -> 1 concrete problem you noticed -> 1 mechanism -> low-friction ask.

## 5. Anti-AI-Slop quality gate (`humanizer` v4.0.0)

Verify all six items before finalizing:

1. Zero `Not X but Y` false contrasts, staged openers (*Let's dive in*), or dramatic one-line closers (*Let that sink in.*).
2. Zero stock AI words (*delve, seamless, robust, crucial, pivotal, landscape, game-changer, comprehensive*).
3. No forced three-item lists unless reality requires three distinct items.
4. Active voice with explicit actors; bloated verbs (*serves as*, *boasts*, *leverages*) replaced with plain verbs.
5. Every claim backed by a real number, concrete mechanism, or marked `[NEED PROOF: ...]`.
6. Minimal em-dashes and clean sentence-case headings without decorative emojis.
