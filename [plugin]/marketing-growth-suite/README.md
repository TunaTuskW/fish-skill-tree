# Marketing, Brand, SEO & Content Engine Suite (`marketing-growth-suite`)

Unified marketing, growth, SEO, brand strategy, and copywriting suite.

## Bundled Skills
- `seo`: Technical SEO (crawlability, indexability, Core Web Vitals), on-page optimization, `schema.org` structured data, and programmatic keyword mapping.
- `brand-voice` & `brand-discovery`: Capture durable brand voice profiles, positioning matrices, and messaging pillars.
- `content-engine` & `crosspost`: Platform-native content systems for X threads, LinkedIn posts, YouTube/short-form video scripts, and newsletters.
- `marketing-campaign`: End-to-end multi-channel product launch campaigns, landing page copy, email arcs, and ad variants.
- `market-research` & `lead-intelligence`: Competitor teardowns, jobs-to-be-done audience profiling, and funnel intelligence.
- `content-writing`: Anti-AI-slop technical, analytical, and product writing (Humanizer + BLUF standard).
