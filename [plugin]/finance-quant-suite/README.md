# Finance, Quant & Accounting Suite (`finance-quant-suite`)

Unified domain suite combining institutional corporate finance, spreadsheet modeling, quantitative mathematics, data science, and prediction market analytics.

## Bundled Skills
- `3-statement-model`: Integrated Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow modeling.
- `accounting-tools`: Excel workbook generation, XIRR/NPV/PMT formulas, and consolidation.
- `audit-xls`: Automated formula error & balance tie-out verification.
- `dcf-model`: Intrinsic equity valuation and WACC sensitivity modeling.
- `lbo-model`: Leveraged buyout modeling and debt schedules.
- `comps-analysis`: Public comparable company multiples and benchmarking.
- `financial-analysis`: SEC filing extraction, GAAP vs. Non-GAAP analysis, and portfolio metrics.
- `finance-billing-ops`: Billing operations, invoicing, and revenue recognition workflows.
- `data-analytics`: Polars, DuckDB, Statsmodels, and Scikit-learn pipelines.
- `math-toolkit`: Symbolic derivations (SymPy), numerical linear algebra, and convex optimization (CVXPY).
- `prediction-market-analytics`, `prediction-market-oracle-research`, `prediction-market-risk-review`: Two-sided prediction market pricing, oracle resolution auditing, and standardized ticket generation.
